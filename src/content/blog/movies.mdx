## Blackfyre is Introduced to Movies

I can't possibly remember which movie was the first one that I watched, but it was definitely something targeting the child public, because I was a kid. I remember watching some like X-Men, Shrek, Harry Potter, Transformers, and the OG Spider-Man.

## Goal

Watching movies was a hobby very present while I was growing up, many of them again watched on TV. I remember Tela Quente from Globo, which had many dope movies. On Saturday and Sunday, we also had some during the afternoon and at the end of the night.

I got a lot of movies on DVD — that was the way to watch the specific ones you wanted to see during those years. Internet wasn't a big thing during that period; we mostly had dial-up internet, which sucked, and most people either didn't have it or couldn't enjoy it properly.

But as time passed, I got access to the internet and to more movies that I could watch. I was a movie person and have some stuff to talk about them. Reviewing stuff and sharing my opinions with others may feel liberating, and making that content reach another person who could become interested in the movie is pretty cool.

## Posts

Each post in the movies category will contain a review with my opinion about it and also some rating. I like to rate movies so I can compare them to others, and maybe that helps with judging other movies and discovering new ones — if that makes sense.

So feel free to read, and I hope you discover something new, are able to relate, or even disagree with some of the stuff I'll say here.

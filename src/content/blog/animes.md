## Blackfyre is Introduced to Animes

The first anime that I watched was probably Yu-Gi-Oh! After that, I got into Dragon Ball, initially hating it. Then, when I grew up, I switched to Zatch Bell!, Naruto, and more...  
It took me 26 years to watch Evangelion. I had been eyeing this anime for so long, and it kinda didn't disappoint. I wasn't expecting something extraordinary; I really liked it, actually, despite the endings being completely crazy.

## Dragon Ball

Definitely the anime that I watched the most, and it used to be my favorite one too. The years passed, and I was introduced to more anime, but my opinion about Dragon Ball didn't change.  
I still remember to this day coming home from school to watch the Cell arc on fucking TV Globinho (RIP). Thanks to TV Globinho, I was introduced to this masterpiece.

Watching episodes out of order on TV kinda sucked. They restarted the arc and changed the order out of nowhere, and you got lost. It was only after some years that I was able to watch the anime properly and follow the continuity.

## Naruto

Naruto used to be on SBT, I think. I didn't watch it on TV. I watched Naruto online; it was the second anime that I got invested in.  
It wasn't even close to Dragon Ball, but I liked it.

I finished only the original Naruto. Naruto Shippuden I dropped during the Madara arc and never came back to it. I got bored, and nothing motivated me to return to watching.

## The Recent Masterpiece

I had been planning to watch Evangelion for a long time. When I finally watched it, I was still surprised by its quality and complete madness. I have a lot to talk about regarding this one, but I have to finish one of the alternative endings, the Rebuild series.  
Although being short, it was really a good way to spend time, watching this anime and adding another one to the collection.
